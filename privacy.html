Privacy policy

LIA is a private application used only by its owner, Tom Petreca, with his own Google account.

  • Gmail: read-only access (gmail.readonly) to the owner's mailbox, used to organize the owner's school communications. LIA never sends, modifies or deletes email.

  • Data stays on the owner's private server. It is not sold, shared or transferred to third parties and is not used for advertising or model training.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
